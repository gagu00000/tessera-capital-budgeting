Independent Assignment: AI-Powered Capital Budgeting and Investment Decision Application
Assignment Overview
In this individual assignment, each student will design and build a simple AI-powered application that helps a business evaluate a real-world capital investment decision. The application should allow a user to enter project information, calculate important capital budgeting measures, compare investment alternatives, and receive an AI-generated recommendation.
The student must also submit a professional report explaining the investment problem, financial calculations, assumptions, risks, results, and final recommendation.
Project Objective
The purpose of this assignment is to apply capital budgeting concepts to a realistic business decision, such as:
•	Purchasing new machinery or equipment
•	Opening a new branch or facility
•	Launching a new product
•	Investing in technology or automation
•	Replacing an old asset
•	Expanding production capacity
•	Installing renewable-energy equipment
•	Developing a new digital platform
The student may select a real company, a small business, a start-up, or create a realistic fictional company.
Part A: AI Application
Build a beginner-friendly application using tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Lovable, Replit, Streamlit, Bubble, Glide, Power Apps, or another suitable platform.
The application must allow the user to enter:
•	Initial investment cost
•	Installation and transportation costs
•	Working capital requirement
•	Project life
•	Expected annual revenues
•	Fixed and variable operating costs
•	Depreciation
•	Tax rate
•	Expected salvage value
•	Required rate of return or cost of capital
The application must calculate and display:
1.	Initial project cash flow
2.	Annual operating cash flows
3.	Terminal-year cash flow
4.	Payback Period
5.	Discounted Payback Period
6.	Accounting Rate of Return
7.	Net Present Value
8.	Internal Rate of Return
9.	Modified Internal Rate of Return
10.	Profitability Index
11.	Break-even analysis
12.	Sensitivity analysis
13.	Best-case, base-case, and worst-case scenarios
The AI component should explain the results in simple language, identify major risks, compare alternative projects, and provide a final recommendation such as Accept, Reject, Delay, or Review Further.
Part B: Written Report
Submit a report of approximately 1,300–1500 words containing:
1.	Company and project background
2.	Description of the investment decision
3.	Project assumptions and data sources
4.	Identification of relevant and irrelevant cash flows
5.	Treatment of sunk costs, opportunity costs, working capital, depreciation, taxes, and salvage value
6.	Capital budgeting calculations and results
7.	Comparison of investment alternatives
8.	Sensitivity and scenario analysis
9.	Financial and non-financial risks
10.	AI-generated insights and student evaluation of those insights
11.	Final investment recommendation
12.	Limitations of the analysis
The report must include appropriate tables, formulas, screenshots of the application, and references.
Required Submission
Submit the following:
•	Working application link or application file
•	Written report in PDF or Word format
•	Reference list and declaration of AI tools used
Important Instructions
•	The project must be completed individually.
•	All assumptions must be realistic and clearly explained.
•	Calculations must be financially correct and independently verified.
•	AI should support the analysis, but the final judgment must be made by the student.
•	Do not rely only on AI-generated numbers or recommendations.
•	Clearly acknowledge all AI tools, data sources, and external references used.
•	The application should be simple enough for a non-finance user to understand.
•	Marks will be awarded for financial accuracy, practical relevance, application functionality, quality of analysis, originality, report presentation, and critical evaluation of AI output.
